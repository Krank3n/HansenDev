## How to Use AI for Small Business Without Wasting Time

The biggest mistake I see operators make is trying to automate their entire business in one weekend. They sign up for ten different platforms, get overwhelmed by the setup, and go back to pen and paper by Tuesday.

*I've spent enough time around Cairns tradies to know that if a tool takes more than five minutes to learn, it goes straight in the bin. When I was building QuoteMate, the first beta tester was a chippy in Gordonvale who refused to use it until I stripped out half the buttons.*

If you want to know how to use ai for small business effectively, pick one single problem. Maybe it is sorting out your receipts for the [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/) before BAS time. Maybe it is writing responses to Google reviews. Fix that one problem first. Once you trust the system and see the time savings, you can move on to the next bottleneck.

**Pro Tip:** Create a basic text file on your phone with instructions for your AI tool. Include details like "Always quote prices inclusive of GST" and "Use a friendly, professional tone." Paste this in every time you ask it to write something so you don't have to repeat yourself.
