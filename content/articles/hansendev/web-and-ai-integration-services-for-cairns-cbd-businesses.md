## Custom Software vs. Off-the-Shelf Solutions

One of the most common questions we encounter in **technology consulting Queensland** is whether to buy existing software (SaaS) or build a custom solution.

### The Case for SaaS (Software as a Service)
If your needs are generic—like basic accounting (Xero) or simple communication (Slack)—off-the-shelf software is cost-effective and reliable.

### The Case for Custom Software Development
However, if your business has unique workflows that provide a competitive advantage, standard software can become a bottleneck. You should consider **custom software development** when:
*   **You are hacking spreadsheets:** If you rely on complex Excel sheets to bridge gaps between different software programs.
*   **You have unique IP:** Your process is your product, and no existing software maps to it.
*   **Licensing costs are scaling poorly:** You have a large team, and per-user licensing fees are becoming exorbitant.

### Hybrid Solutions
Often, the best approach is a hybrid one. HansenDev specializes in building custom integrations that connect your existing tools. For example, connecting your website's lead form directly to your project management board using an API.
