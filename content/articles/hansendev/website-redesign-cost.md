A tour operator down at the Marlin Marina recently told me he missed out on a $4,000 group booking because his contact form crashed on an iPhone. The customer got frustrated, went back to Google, and booked with a competitor. When he asked me about the website redesign cost to fix his outdated setup, he was bracing for a massive number. 

Most business owners treat web development like taking their ute to the mechanic. You know something is making a funny noise, but you are terrified of what the final invoice will look like. The truth is, pricing in this industry varies wildly. You can find someone on the internet offering to do it for $500, and you can find a big-city agency quoting $50,000 for the exact same brief.

Here is a flat look at what you should actually expect to pay to get your site fixed, functional, and generating leads.

## TL;DR

* Basic template face-lifts for small businesses start around $2,500.
* Standard custom redesigns typically range from $5,000 to $15,000.
* Complex builds and e-commerce platforms push beyond $15,000.
* Writing the copy yourself is the biggest hidden delay in any project.
* A proper redesign pays for itself quickly through recovered lost leads.

## The Reality of the Cost to Redesign Website

The hardest part about quoting a digital project is that you are paying for invisible labor. When you hire a chippy to build a deck, you can see the Merbau timber and the screws. When you hire a developer, you are paying for lines of code, database architecture, and user experience planning. 

*I've spent enough time around Cairns tradies to know that nobody wants to pay for tech they don't need. When I was quoting a job for a sparkie up in Edmonton last month, he just wanted his phone to ring. We stripped back the bells and whistles, focused entirely on mobile load speed, and kept his bill under three grand.*

The cost to redesign website projects comes down to scope. If you just need a digital business card that looks good on a phone, your costs will be low. If you need a system that integrates with your CRM, takes deposits, and manages customer accounts, the price scales up with the complexity.

## Breakdown: What Does a Website Redesign Cost?

To give you a realistic idea of the cost for website redesign, we need to break the market into three distinct tiers. All figures here are ex-GST and represent standard Australian agency rates.

### Tier 1: The Basic Face-Lift ($2,500 - $5,000)
This is the sweet spot for most local service businesses. Think plumbers, electricians, or a boutique shop in Palm Cove that just needs to show their opening hours and services. 

At this price point, developers use an existing premium framework or product like our WebFaceLift service. We take your current content, map it to a modern, mobile-responsive layout, and make sure it loads fast. You aren't getting weeks of custom graphic design. You are getting a clean, functional tool that stops you from losing customers to broken mobile layouts.

### Tier 2: The Standard Business Redesign ($5,000 - $15,000)
This is the full website redesign cost for an established business that needs to stand out. An accounting firm in the Cairns CBD or a medium-sized real estate agency usually lands in this bracket.

Here, you are paying for strategy. The agency will look at your user journey, wireframe custom page layouts, and build a site specifically tailored to your brand. It includes technical SEO foundations, custom post types for your specific services, and integration with your booking software or mailing list.

### Tier 3: The Complex or E-Commerce Build ($15,000+)
If you are selling thousands of products online or need a custom portal for your clients, the price goes up. E-commerce requires secure payment gateways, variable shipping calculators, and inventory management. 

These projects take hundreds of hours of development and testing. You are essentially building a digital storefront that operates 24/7, and the infrastructure needs to be rock solid.

## The BOM Website Redesign Cost Explained

Clients sometimes ask me about crazy government tech budgets they see in the news. A few years ago, the Bureau of Meteorology overhauled their digital presence, and people still search for the bom website redesign cost. It ran into the tens of millions of dollars.

When you see a number like that, it is easy to think the whole industry is a rort. But enterprise and government systems are a different sport entirely. They handle millions of concurrent users during weather emergencies. They require military-grade security, massive server clusters, and integration with global satellite data. 

Your small business site does not need this. You just need clean code and a reliable server. Don't let enterprise horror stories scare you away from updating your own digital presence.

## Hidden Fees in the Cost of Website Redesign

The quote from your web developer is rarely the final number on the spreadsheet. The actual cost of website redesign involves a few other critical elements that catch business owners off guard.

### Copywriting and Content
You look at a blank Word document and think, *"Yeah, I'll just write the about page and the service descriptions this weekend."* Six weeks later, you have been flat out on the tools, the site is delayed, and your developer is waiting on you. 

Hiring a professional copywriter costs between $1,000 and $3,000 for a standard site. It is almost always worth the investment. They write words that actually convert visitors into paying customers.

### Professional Photography
Do not use stock photos of American tradies driving left-hand drive trucks. Your customers can spot them a mile away. 

Hire a local photographer for a half-day shoot. Get photos of your ute, your team, and your actual projects around FNQ. It will add $500 to $1,000 to your budget, but it builds immediate trust with locals.

**Pro Tip:** If you are migrating a site that already gets good Google traffic, ensure your developer includes a 301 redirect map in their quote. This tells Google where your old pages moved to. If they skip this, you will lose your search rankings overnight.

## How Developers Decide How Much to Charge for Website Redesign

If you are wondering how agencies figure out how much to charge for website redesign, the math is actually very boring. It is just estimated hours multiplied by an hourly rate.

A senior Australian developer costs between $120 and $180 per hour. If an agency estimates your custom redesign will take 40 hours of design, 40 hours of development, and 10 hours of project management, you are looking at 90 hours total. At $150 an hour, that is a $13,500 quote. 

When someone quotes you $800 for that same job, they are either outsourcing the work overseas, using a pirated template, or planning to disappear halfway through the project. Good custom software development takes time, and time costs money.

## How Much Does Website Redesign Cost in Lost Leads?

The conversation around price usually focuses on the money leaving your bank account. It rarely focuses on the money failing to enter it.

If your site takes seven seconds to load on a 4G connection in the Atherton Tablelands, people will hit the back button. If your contact form is broken, they will call the next business on the search results. 

A $5,000 redesign might sound like a big hit to cash flow. But if your average job is worth $2,000, and your new site stops you from losing three jobs a month, the project pays for itself in less than thirty days. You can also check with the [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/) regarding the instant asset write-off, as digital assets often qualify for immediate tax deductions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What does a website redesign cost for a basic tradie site?
For a standard service business that needs 5 to 7 pages (Home, About, Services, Contact), expect to pay between $2,500 and $5,000 ex-GST. This covers a modern, mobile-friendly design that loads fast and looks professional.

### Q: How long does a full redesign take?
A basic face-lift takes 2 to 4 weeks, provided you have your text and photos ready. A full custom redesign or e-commerce build usually takes 8 to 12 weeks from the initial strategy meeting to the final launch.

### Q: Will I lose my Google rankings during a redesign?
You shouldn't, provided your developer handles the SEO migration correctly. They need to map your old URLs to your new ones using 301 redirects. Always ask if SEO migration is included in your quote.

### Q: Do I need to pay monthly fees after the redesign?
Yes. Every website needs hosting, domain renewal, and software updates to stay secure. Expect to pay between $50 and $150 a month for reliable Australian hosting and routine maintenance.
