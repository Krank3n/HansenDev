## Why Tradies Need Quoting AND Invoicing in One App

If you're using separate tools for quoting and invoicing, you're losing time and money in at least three ways:

### 1. Double Data Entry

Every time you re-type a client's name, address, and job details into a separate invoice tool, that's 10-15 minutes of unpaid admin. Multiply that by 20 jobs a month and you're looking at 5+ hours of mindless copy-paste work.

### 2. Pricing Discrepancies

When your quote lives in one system and your invoice in another, it's easy for numbers to drift. Maybe you updated the material quantities during the job but forgot to reflect that on the invoice. Or the client approved a variation but it only exists in a text message. One system means one source of truth.

### 3. Jobs Falling Through the Cracks

When quoting and invoicing are disconnected, it's surprisingly easy to finish a job and forget to invoice it entirely. A **quote and invoice software** that links the two ensures every accepted quote has a corresponding invoice.

## How the New Invoice Feature Works

QuoteMate's invoice system is built on the same principles that make the quoting side great: speed, simplicity, and professional output.

### Creating an Invoice from Scratch

If you need to invoice for a job that didn't start as a QuoteMate quote, you can create a standalone invoice:

1. **Tap "New Invoice"** from the dashboard.
2. **Add your client details** — or select an existing client from your contacts.
3. **Add line items** — materials with real-time **Bunnings prices**, labour hours, and any extras.
4. **Set payment terms** — due on receipt, 7 days, 14 days, or 30 days.
5. **Generate and send** — a professional PDF invoice is emailed to your client in seconds.

### Converting a Quote to an Invoice (The Game Changer)

This is where the real magic happens. Once a client accepts your quote:

1. **Open the accepted quote.**
2. **Tap "Convert to Invoice."**
3. **Review** — all line items, materials, labour, and client details carry across automatically.
4. **Adjust if needed** — add any variations or extras that came up during the job.
5. **Send** — done. What used to take 20 minutes now takes 20 seconds.

The invoice retains the original quote number as a reference, so you and your client can always trace the invoice back to the agreed scope. No arguments, no confusion.

## Tracking Your Invoices

Sending an invoice is only half the battle. Knowing which ones have been paid, which are overdue, and how much you're owed at any given moment is critical for managing your cash flow.

### The Invoice Dashboard

QuoteMate's invoice tracking gives you a clear picture:

*   **Draft** — invoices you haven't sent yet
*   **Sent** — invoices delivered to the client
*   **Paid** — invoices that have been settled
*   **Overdue** — invoices past their due date (highlighted in red so they're impossible to miss)

At a glance, you can see your total outstanding amount and which clients owe you money. No more guessing whether that deck build from three weeks ago has been paid.

### Recording Payments

When money hits your account, recording the payment is simple:

1. **Open the invoice.**
2. **Tap "Record Payment."**
3. **Enter the amount** — supports full and partial payments.
4. **Done** — the invoice status updates automatically.

For jobs with progress payments (like a 50% deposit and 50% on completion), QuoteMate tracks each instalment so you always know the remaining balance.
