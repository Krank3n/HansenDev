## The Hidden Cost of "Guesstimating" Materials

We’ve all done it. You’re on site, looking at a potential job, and you do a quick mental calculation. "Yeah, reckon that's about $500 in materials." You scribble it on a notepad or text it to the client.

This approach is costing you thousands of dollars a year.

### The "Overs and Unders" Trap
When you guess, you usually do one of two things:
1.  **Underquote:** You win the job, but you work for free because materials cost more than expected.
2.  **Overquote:** You add a huge "safety buffer," making your price too high, and you lose the job to a competitor using a sharper **quote builder Australia** relies on.

### The Time Drain
Even if you are diligent and sit down with a supplier website to check every screw and plank, how long does that take? For a complex deck or renovation, you could be spending 2-3 hours just looking up prices. That’s time you could spend with your family or on the tools earning money.

**Pro Tip:** Your time is billable. If you spend 5 hours a week quoting for free, you are losing billable hours. Automating this with a **job estimator** pays for itself immediately.

## Beyond Materials: Managing Labour and Margins

While nailing your **Bunnings prices** is crucial, a profitable quote needs to account for your sweat equity.

### Setting Your Margin
A common mistake is adding a markup to materials but underestimating labour. A good **builder estimator** tool allows you to:
1.  Set a default markup on materials (e.g., 10-20% for handling/delivery).
2.  Input your hourly labour rate or a fixed project fee.

### The "Fudge Factor"
Always allow for waste. When using a **fence quote app** or calculating flooring, you need that extra 10% for cuts and waste. QuoteMate’s AI typically factors this in, but being able to manually adjust quantities ensures you don’t run short on the job.
