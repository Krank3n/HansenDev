## The True Cost of Manual Quoting

Most tradies look at admin time as "free" work they do after hours. This is a dangerous mindset for your business. To understand why you need a **quote builder in Australia**, you have to do the math on your time.

### The $400 Weekly Loss
Let's say your charge-out rate is $80 per hour. If you spend one hour each weeknight or a 5-hour block on Sunday doing paperwork, that is 5 hours of unpaid labor.

*   **5 hours x $80/hr = $400 per week.**
*   **$400 x 50 weeks = $20,000 per year.**

That is $20,000 of lost billable time, or $20,000 worth of family time you are giving away for free. By cutting that time in half using efficient software, you essentially give yourself a $10,000 pay rise.

### The Opportunity Cost
It’s not just about the hours you lose; it’s about the jobs you miss. If a client asks for **carpenter quotes** for a new deck and you take four days to reply because you're swamped, you’ve likely already lost the job. Speed wins contracts.

**Pro Tip:** Treat your admin time with the same strictness as your on-site time. If a task takes longer than 15 minutes, look for a way to automate it.
